Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Overview

TidyNest Finds is a private, single-user content management tool developed for personal use. This application is not offered as a public service or to multiple users.

Information and Account Access

The application may use Pinterest's API and OAuth authorization to connect to and publish content to the owner's Pinterest account. Access is used only for the functionality authorized by the account owner.

Use of Information

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to operate the application's Pinterest publishing functionality, including creating and publishing Pins that have been manually reviewed and approved.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or rent personal information and does not share Pinterest account information with third parties except where necessary to provide the application's intended functionality.

Data Storage and Security

The application is operated privately on a personal computer. Reasonable measures are taken to protect credentials and information used by the application.

Data Deletion

Because this is a private, single-user application, the owner can remove locally stored application data and revoke the application's access to the Pinterest account at any time.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the owner of TidyNest Finds.
