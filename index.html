About the App

Habitique is a private content management tool used to create, review, and publish Pinterest content for Habitique.

The application helps organize product-related Pinterest content, prepare Pin descriptions and destination links, and publish content to the connected Pinterest account after manual approval.

This application is intended for private, single-user use.
